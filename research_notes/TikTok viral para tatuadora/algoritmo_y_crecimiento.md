# TikTok Algorithm (2025-2026) and New-Account Growth

Research date: 2026-10-07. Source quality is marked throughout. "OFFICIAL" means TikTok's own newsroom or support pages. Most other numbers come from marketing tool vendors such as Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout and Socialinsider. Many smaller "2026 guides" are low-quality SEO content and are flagged where they are used.

## 1. Which ranking signals does the For You feed weigh?

### Takeaway
TikTok's official position has not changed. Ranking uses three signal groups: user interactions (strongest), video information, and device/account settings (weakest). TikTok explicitly says follower count and past viral hits are not direct factors. TikTok does not publish signal weights. Marketers agree that watch time, completion rate, rewatches and shares lead, followed by saves, comments and follows. Likes are weaker.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL: The For You feed ranks videos using three groups of signals:
  - User interactions: likes, shares, accounts followed, comments posted, content created.
  - Video information: captions, sounds, hashtags.
  - Device and account settings: language, country, device type. These get lower weight "because users don't actively express them as preferences."
  - [TikTok Newsroom – How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- OFFICIAL: Signals are weighted by how strongly they indicate interest. TikTok's own example is that finishing a longer video is a stronger signal than viewer and creator being in the same country. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- OFFICIAL: "Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors." TikTok also says a larger account may still get more views indirectly because it has more followers. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- OFFICIAL: The feed avoids showing two videos in a row with the same sound or from the same creator. It does not recommend duplicated content, content the user has already seen, or spam. TikTok also mixes in videos with few likes or from outside the user's stated interests. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- Hootsuite (article dated June 1, 2026) gives an approximate signal hierarchy:
  - Strongest: watch time, completion rate, replays, shares.
  - Strong: comments, follows from a video, saves.
  - Moderate: likes, hashtag engagement.
  - Weak: device, language, location.
  - Hootsuite says TikTok has never confirmed a point system, so claims like "1 point per like, 5 per share" are unverified.
  - [Hootsuite – TikTok algorithm](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Hootsuite: the algorithm also reads on-screen text and spoken words as video information. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Hootsuite cites TikTok internal data that "high-quality creators" get 72% more watch time per view and more than 40x greater follower growth. TikTok's definition of "high-quality" is not given. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Marketing sources disagree on which signal is #1:
  - Watch time is #1: [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/tiktok-algorithm-ranking-signals) and [eClincher 2026](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026).
  - Saves and shares outweigh likes "significantly": [Framia](https://framia.converge.ai/blog/tiktok-algorithm/).
  - Both are vendor blogs with no primary data.
- Reported for 2026 (vendor claim, unverified): creators who post consistently in one niche gain "topic authority," which improves distribution. [eClincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Shares and saves come up repeatedly as strong signals. For a portfolio-type creator such as a tattoo artist, content people want to save as a reference ("save this for your next tattoo") or send to a friend is likely the most efficient lever after retention.
- Since follower count is not a direct factor, a new account is not structurally locked out. Each video is judged mostly on how its test audience reacts.

### Gaps
- TikTok has not published official signal weights. Any numeric weighting found online is speculative.
- I found no official TikTok statement on whether profile visits or search-originated views are separate ranking signals. Marketers assert this without evidence.
- The TikTok support page "How TikTok recommends content" (now at tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7655285288050104852) could not be read because it renders client-side.

## 2. How does distribution work for new accounts? Test batches, second wave, evergreen content, warm-up

### Takeaway
The commonly described pipeline goes from a small test audience (a few hundred users) to progressively larger pools if engagement crosses thresholds. It is plausible and widely repeated, but TikTok has never published batch sizes or thresholds. Evidence for a "new creator boost" and for "account warm-up" is anecdotal only.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor description, no primary data: the initial test group is roughly 200-500 users and is more random for new accounts. If engagement passes thresholds, the video expands to 5,000-50,000+ users. Otherwise distribution stops. [Search summary of blckalpaca / Sendcove guides](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/tiktok-algorithm-for-you-page.md); [Sendcove](https://www.sendcove.app/integrations/tiktok/algorithm-explained)
- An unverified 2026 guide claims the first batch goes largely to the account's existing audience, and that reactions in the first 30-90 minutes set the reach ceiling. No other source corroborates this. [eClincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026)
- OFFICIAL: newly uploaded videos and videos under review may be temporarily ineligible for recommendation. This explains the near-zero views some posts get in their first minutes. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- Anecdote (January 2023, older and n=1): a new account posted one video a day for 10 days. Views ran 547, 546, 580, 560, then 20, 90 and so on. This fits either a "new account boost" or a baseline test pool of about 500 viewers that later posts failed to exceed. [Indie Hackers experiment](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tiktok-experiment-does-it-artificially-boost-posts-of-new-accounts-heres-the-view-count-3aac9b1882)
- Buffer (11.4M posts, 150K+ accounts) reports a median of about 459-489 views per post regardless of posting frequency. This is consistent with a baseline test pool of a few hundred viewers. [Buffer – How often to post on TikTok](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
- Warm-up and the "new account" phase: creator blogs say low early reach on new accounts is normal and lasts "a few days to a week." This is often mistaken for a shadowban. No official confirmation exists. [Virlo glossary](https://virlo.ai/glossary/tiktok-shadowban); [Loomly](https://www.loomly.com/blog/tiktok-shadowban)
- Growth timelines (vendor observation, not a study): accounts that reached 100K+ followers typically took 3-12 months of consistent posting. [Dataslayer](https://dataslayer.ai/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- Evergreen resurfacing:
  - OFFICIAL: the Nearby feed shows posts up to 90 days old. [TikTok Newsroom – Nearby](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-nearby-discover-whats-happening-around-you)
  - Search traffic can keep surfacing older videos. See section 5.

### Inferences
- In practice, every video gets a fresh test. For a new account, a few hundred views per video is the normal baseline, not a penalty. Breakouts come from the 90th-percentile video, not the median one (see the Buffer data in section 3).
- "Warm-up" rituals (scrolling your niche for days before posting, liking competitors' videos) have no official basis. The most they can plausibly do is tune your own For You feed so you learn the niche's trends. That is useful for research, not for reach.

### Gaps
- There are no official numbers for test-batch sizes, thresholds or "second wave" timing.
- I found no rigorous study of a "new creator boost." The only evidence is anecdotal.
- I found no data on how often old videos go viral later. The phenomenon is widely reported anecdotally but not quantified.

## 3. Posting frequency, video length, posting times, consistency vs. volume, niche

### Takeaway
The best 2025-2026 data (Buffer, 11.4M posts) shows that going from 1 to 2-5 posts per week gives the biggest per-post lift (+17%). More volume mainly raises the odds of an outlier hit, not the typical video's views. Posting-time studies contradict each other, so a creator's own analytics should decide. On length, 1-3 minute videos get distributed if retention holds, but short videos remain best for reach.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer frequency study (11.4M posts, 150K+ accounts; each account compared with itself over time):
  - Per-post views compared with posting once a week: 2-5 posts/week give up to +17%, 6-10 give up to +29%, 11+ give up to +34%.
  - Median views barely move: 489 at 1 post/week vs. 459 at 11+.
  - 90th-percentile views rise sharply: 3,722 vs. 14,401.
  - Buffer's data scientist: "Posting more helps – but mostly because it increases your chances of getting lucky."
  - [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/); [Social Media Today coverage](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-post-frequency-improves-per-post-reach-report-buffer/802443/)
- Buffer best times (7.1M posts, published September 14, 2026; times adjusted to apply in any local time zone):
  - Top slots: Sunday 9 a.m., Monday 1 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m.
  - Best days: Saturday, then Monday, then Sunday.
  - Evenings (6-11 p.m.) tend to peak. Afternoons (12-5 p.m.) are lowest on most days. Wednesday and Thursday are quieter.
  - [Buffer – Best time to post on TikTok](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/)
- Sprout Social (2B engagements, 307K profiles, data from November 2025 to February 2026, seen via secondary write-ups only): weekday afternoons work well, especially Monday 3-5 p.m. [Search summary; e.g. Descript](https://descript.com/blog/article/best-times-to-post-on-tiktok)
- Hootsuite / Critical Truth: Thursday morning or Saturday midday. This conflicts with Buffer and Sprout. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Length:
  - Hootsuite (2026): TikTok has been distributing longer videos (1-3+ minutes) when completion stays high. In-app recording allows up to 10 minutes, and uploads up to 60 minutes. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
  - SocialPilot (1.5M posts): 31-60 second videos get the highest engagement, while videos under 30 seconds are better for broad reach and discovery. [SocialPilot via search](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/blog/tiktok-video-size)
  - Metricool 2024 study (older): 2-5 minute videos had the highest average views (about 50K). [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-study)
  - Claimed from Socialinsider 2025 (I could not verify it on Socialinsider's page): videos with more than 50% average completion get more distribution regardless of length. [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/best-tiktok-video-lengths-for-creators-in-2026)
- Monetization context (third-party sources): the Creator Rewards Program pays only for videos longer than 1 minute. A "qualified view" requires at least 5 seconds of watch time and must come from the For You feed. Eligibility requires 10K followers and 18+. This pushes many creators toward videos over 1 minute. [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/tiktok-creativity-program); [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/creator-rewards-program-review-57835/)
- Engagement benchmarks (Socialinsider, H1 2025):
  - Average engagement rate is 4.90%, up 12%.
  - Accounts under 5K followers have the highest engagement rate by views, 4.20%.
  - [Socialinsider TikTok benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok)
  - Hootsuite gives the average brand engagement rate as 2.1%. The methodology differs. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Niche focus: the claimed "topic authority" reward is described in section 1 (vendor claim). [eClincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026)

### Inferences
- For a new solo creator, about 3-5 posts per week is the sustainable sweet spot. Pushing toward daily posting buys more lottery tickets rather than higher typical reach. Consistency matters more than bursts.
- Posting-time studies disagree almost completely (Buffer: evenings and weekends; Sprout: Monday afternoon; Hootsuite: Thursday morning). Posting time is a second-order factor. Use the "Followers > activity" chart in TikTok Analytics once the account has data.
- A mixed length strategy fits the data: 7-30 second hooks and transformations for reach, plus 60-90+ second process or storytime videos for watch time and search.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve Sprout's original 2026 report.
- I found no length study segmented for new or small accounts.

## 4. Hooks: the first 1-3 seconds, formulas, retention thresholds

### Takeaway
TikTok's Creator Academy says "the first three seconds are critical" (as quoted by Hootsuite). The popular hard numbers, such as "63% of top ads deliver the message in 3 seconds" or "lose 60% in 3 seconds and you die," could not be traced to a primary source and come mostly from ads data. Typical hook rates (3-second views divided by impressions) run about 20-35%. Above 50% is considered excellent.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Creator Academy, as quoted by Hootsuite: "the first three seconds are critical." [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- More than 30% of TikTok users watch on mute. This argues for on-screen text hooks and captions. [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- "63% of top-performing ads deliver the core message within 3 seconds" is attributed to a TikTok review of ads. It is UNVERIFIED (no link or methodology) and concerns ads, not organic posts. [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/hook-rate-to-hold-rate)
- Hook-rate benchmarks (vendor):
  - 20-35% is standard for creator accounts, and more than 50% is excellent. [NestScale](https://nestscale.com/?p=47200)
  - Motion reports an average hook rate of about 25-30% across short-form video ads. [Motion via NestScale](https://nestscale.com/?p=47200)
  - Hook rate is defined as 3-second views divided by impressions.
- "Videos that lose 60%+ of viewers in the first 3 seconds rarely reach the broader FYP" comes from a vendor blog with no evidence. Treat it as a heuristic only. [Search summary; tlinky](https://tlinky.com/3-second-hook/)
- "Strong hooks give about 60% higher retention" comes from unnamed research. Unverified. [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/why-the-first-few-seconds-matter)

### Inferences
These are practitioner hook formulas, adapted for a tattoo artist. They are not individually sourced; they are standard creator-economy patterns.
- Show the result first, then rewind ("Look how this turned out" over the healed piece, then cut to the stencil).
- Open loop or promise ("Wait for the color pass" / "Watch until the end to see her reaction").
- Text-on-screen question or controversy ("Fine line tattoos DON'T last? Let's see 2 years later").
- Visual pattern interrupt in frame 1: an extreme close-up of the needle, a fast before/after wipe, or movement in the first frame.
- Negative or mistake framing ("3 things that ruin your tattoo in the first week").
- Measure in TikTok Analytics: the retention curve, % of viewers who watched the full video, and average watch time. Compare openings across posts.

### Gaps
- I found no official TikTok organic retention threshold (for example, "X% completion unlocks the next batch").
- The primary source for the 63% figure was not found.

## 5. TikTok SEO and search: captions, on-screen text, speech, hashtags, local discovery

### Takeaway
TikTok indexes caption text, on-screen text, auto-captions/speech and hashtags. Vendors agree that search-style keywords in the caption and spoken in the first seconds help discovery. Hashtags were capped at 5 per post for many users from mid-2025. Use 3-5 specific, relevant tags. For local businesses, the new Nearby feed (December 2025, starting in the UK, France, Germany and Italy) ranks by location, topic and recency.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL: captions, sounds and hashtags are "video information" signals. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- TikTok scans caption text, on-screen text overlays, hashtags and auto-generated speech-to-text to understand video topic. This is a vendor claim consistent with Hootsuite. [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/social-search-how-to-optimize-for-discoverability-on-tiktok-and-instagram-2025); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- A reported "Search Value" signal analyzes auto-captions and text overlays to match videos with search queries. This is a vendor claim. [Framia](https://framia.converge.ai/blog/tiktok-algorithm/)
- 84% of TikTok searches happen in an "exploration phase" (eMarketer, via Hootsuite). [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- The widely repeated "40% of Gen Z prefer TikTok over Google" has no primary survey link in the vendor blogs that cite it. Treat it as unverified. [Search summary; SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-seo)
- Hashtag cap:
  - From July-August 2025, users began seeing a "Maximum 5 hashtags" message when adding a sixth. The rollout appears staged and per-user. [Distractify, August 2025](https://www.distractify.com/p/why-is-tiktok-limiting-my-hashtags); [Planoly, updated November 2025](https://api.planoly.com/blog/tiktok-5-hashtag-limit); [Seoteric](https://www.seoteric.com/tiktok-limits-hashtags-to-five-per-post-what-it-means-for-creators-and-marketers/)
  - Conflict: Sendcove (April 2026) still says "up to 30," with 3-5 recommended. Check in the app. [Sendcove](https://www.sendcove.app/integrations/tiktok/character-limits)
  - I found no official TikTok announcement of the cap.
- Hashtag type: mix specific niche tags with a few broader ones, and avoid irrelevant trending tags. This is vendor advice. [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/how-to-use-hashtags-on-tiktok-in-2026-maximize-your-tiktok-reach-and-engagement.md)
- OFFICIAL, Nearby feed (announced December 4, 2025):
  - Launched in France, Germany, Italy and the UK.
  - Ranks by where posts are made or tagged, topic, and recency.
  - Public posts can reach people in the city or region where the creator posted or tagged a location or service.
  - Not eligible: posts more than 90 days old, accounts under 18, private accounts, and Friends-only or Only-me posts.
  - Location sharing is optional, adults only.
  - TikTok cites 2023 Oxford Economics data that 46% of UK users visited a local business they saw on TikTok.
  - [TikTok Newsroom – Nearby](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-nearby-discover-whats-happening-around-you); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/tiktok-introduces-nearby-feed-boosting-local-discovery-organic-reach); [TechCrunch via scour](https://scour.ing/@matmat/p/https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/04/tiktok-rolls-out-a-nearby-feed-to-display-local-content-in-select-countries)
- Location and category tags reportedly help classification, and Q&A answers reportedly appear in search. These are vendor claims. [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/social-search-how-to-optimize-for-discoverability-on-tiktok-and-instagram-2025)

### Inferences
- For a local tattoo artist, put the search phrase in all three places: say it, show it as on-screen text, and write it in the caption. Example phrases: "tatuajes fine line en [ciudad]," "tatuaje minimalista mujer," "cuidado de tatuaje nuevo."
- Always tag the studio location. Even where Nearby isn't live, location helps topical and local classification.
- Choose 3-5 hashtags: 1 local (#tatuajes[ciudad]), 2-3 style or niche (#fineline, #tatuajesminimalistas), and at most 1 broad tag.

### Gaps
- I found no official TikTok SEO ranking documentation and no independent study measuring the lift from caption keywords.
- I could not confirm whether the Nearby feed has expanded beyond the 4 European markets, for example to Latin America or the US, by October 2026.

## 6. Other formats: carousels, series, Lives, Stories, duets/stitches, audio, video replies to comments

### Takeaway
The data is thin and mostly vendor-generated. Photo carousels show higher engagement rates than video for small accounts in several datasets. TikTok cites that users like a mix of images and video. I found no credible 2025-2026 quantitative data on Lives, Stories, Series, duets/stitches, trending vs. original audio, or video replies to comments.

### Cited Findings
- Carousels:
  - Small brands post more carousels: 3% of their content, vs. 1% for the largest accounts. [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok)
  - Carousels get 15-35% higher engagement rates than video for accounts under 100K followers, and 5-15% higher for larger accounts. Sample of 800+ accounts; third-party, methodology unclear. [TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/tiktok-photo-carousel-engagement/)
  - Metricool 2024 (older): images and carousels beat videos' engagement ratio by more than 25%. [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-study); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20241118-metricool-releases-2024-tiktok-benchmark-study-videos-versus-images-and-ca-1)
  - 76% of users enjoy a mix of images and video (TikTok data via Hootsuite). [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Audio:
  - OFFICIAL: sounds are a ranking input ("video information").
  - OFFICIAL: the feed avoids showing consecutive videos with the same sound, which tempers the "trending audio = guaranteed reach" belief.
  - [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- Q&A and comment replies reportedly appear in search results (vendor). [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/social-search-how-to-optimize-for-discoverability-on-tiktok-and-instagram-2025)

### Inferences
- Carousels are a low-effort test format for a tattoo portfolio (flash sheets, healed vs. fresh, "5 ideas for…"). They may show inflated engagement rates partly because likes and swipes count relative to fewer impressions.
- Replying to comments with videos is a reliable content-idea generator. It produces search-friendly Q&A content ("¿Duele tatuarse en las costillas?").

### Gaps
- I found no credible 2025-2026 data on the reach effects of Lives, Stories, Series (paid collections), duets/stitches, or original vs. trending audio. Treat any claims about these as anecdotal.

## 7. Common mistakes that kill reach; shadowban myth vs. reality

### Takeaway
TikTok officially excludes duplicated or unoriginal content and spam from recommendation, and keeps For You Feed eligibility standards. Reposting other-platform videos with watermarks, re-uploading, and guideline-borderline content are the real reach killers. "Shadowban" is not an official TikTok term. Most perceived shadowbans are ordinary underperformance or the new-account baseline. The claim that deleting videos hurts the account is unsupported either way.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL: the For You feed does not recommend duplicated content, spam, or content that has been "reviewed as unsuitable for general audiences." [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- Community Guidelines update (2024-2025):
  - Unoriginal and low-quality content gets pushed away from the For You feed.
  - Re-uploads from another platform can be excluded from recommendation.
  - Static-image videos with another platform's watermark are deprioritized.
  - Advice: edit each video natively rather than cross-posting Reels as-is.
  - [Online Optimism](https://onlineoptimism.com/blog/updates-tiktoks-community-guidelines)
  - The FYF standards page itself (tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards) could not be fetched.
- Other reported triggers: guideline-borderline content, unlicensed or copyrighted music, spam-like behavior (mass follow/unfollow, comment spam), and mass reports. These are vendor and low-quality sources. [Loomly](https://www.loomly.com/blog/tiktok-shadowban); [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/glossary/tiktok-shadowban)
- "Most perceived shadowbans are actually content performance issues rather than deliberate suppression" is a vendor opinion. [go-viral](https://www.go-viral.app/blog/shadowban-myths-debunked/)
- Deleting videos: sources conflict.
  - Some claim deleting and re-uploading triggers suppression. Others recommend deleting violating videos.
  - There is no official guidance. Unverified.
  - [aivideocut](https://www.aivideocut.com/blog/tiktok-shadow-ban-algorithm); [Loomly](https://www.loomly.com/blog/tiktok-shadowban)
- Diagnosis: check whether For You traffic in Analytics drops suddenly. TikTok also shows notices in the app when a specific video is ineligible for the For You feed. [Search summary; Loomly](https://www.loomly.com/blog/tiktok-shadowban)

### Inferences
- Tattoo-specific risk: realistic close-ups of needles, blood or fresh broken skin, and nudity-adjacent placements (chest, hips) may get flagged as graphic or sexual and limited. Keep frames clean (wipe the blood, crop sensitive areas) and avoid sexualized framing.
- Never post Instagram-watermarked Reels. Export the clean file, or re-edit inside TikTok/CapCut.
- Instead of deleting flops, set underperforming videos to private if needed. Deleting has no proven benefit.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok documentation on whether deleting videos affects account standing.
- No official numbers on how many violations or strikes trigger account-level For You feed ineligibility (TikTok's support pages could not be fetched).
- No official TikTok statement on body-modification or tattoo imagery specifically. This needs review of the current Community Guidelines on graphic content.
